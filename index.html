Common Questions

Everything you need to know about security and usability.

Is my financial data safe?
Yes. By default, this tool runs entirely on your local browser. No data, names, or values are transmitted to our servers. Your database lives in browser `localStorage`.
Do I have to register an account?
No. You can open the application dashboard and start calculating shifts and importing CSV records immediately without registering. Registration is only required if you explicitly choose to sync data across a team using Cloud Workspace Mode.
What happens if I clear my browser cache?
Since your data is local to your browser storage, clearing the cache or browsing in incognito mode will erase records. We recommend regular CSV backups using the 'Export CSV' button on the dashboard. Alternatively, connect a free Cloud Vault account for permanent automatic syncing.
